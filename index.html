Airmai Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026

Airmai uses account information, game records and device capabilities to provide score viewing, synchronization, arcade discovery, chart tools and attendance records.

Accounts and game records

Account information and authorizations are used to request or synchronize records with the data service you select. Airmai stores account state, authorization information, record caches and preferences on your device. OAuth access and refresh tokens are saved in the system Keychain.

Services such as Diving-Fish, LXNS, MuNET and ScoreHub receive the requests required for their features. QR codes, friend codes and authorization links are processed through the synchronization flow you start. Each service handles information under its own policies and terms.

Location and arcade searches

After location permission is granted, Airmai uses your location to find nearby arcades and calculate distances. Nearby searches send latitude and longitude to Nearcade. Arcade search terms are sent with search requests. Apple Maps or your selected maps application handles map display and navigation.

Camera, photos and files

The camera reads machine QR codes. Images you select are used for app and widget backgrounds. Photo-library write permission is used when saving score images. Downloaded charts, audio and imported files are stored in the app directory. You control exports and sharing.

Apple Watch and health data

With health authorization, attendance recording collects heart-rate samples and session times. Apple Watch transfers records to its paired iPhone, where they are stored locally and associated with song records when matching time information is available. Score summaries also synchronize from the phone to the watch.

Health records are processed on the device and its paired device. You can manage health authorization in system settings.

Usage statistics

This release enables anonymous usage statistics by default to help understand device compatibility and commonly used features. The fields are limited to device model, operating-system and app version, feature identifiers, access and action counts, session times and duration. Statistics use an independent random installation identifier.

Statistics are sent to Airmai's statistics service over HTTPS. The pending queue is encrypted on the device. Raw events are retained for up to 30 days, and anonymous aggregates for up to 365 days.

For questions about statistics, authorizations or data handling, contact the maintainer using the address below. You choose which screenshots and reproduction details to include when sending feedback.

Local storage and platforms

Airmai caches artwork, song catalogs, records and downloads. Keychain, system backups and health-data retention are managed by their respective system services. App removal and third-party authorization revocation are handled separately by iOS and the relevant service.

GitHub handles downloads and submitted issues under its privacy policy. Apple handles TestFlight installations, sessions, crashes and submitted feedback according to its TestFlight terms and privacy information.
