Airmai Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Airmai respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Airmai.

1. Information We Collect

Airmai does not actively collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Some features may require you to provide an API key, account information, or other credentials for third-party services. This information is used only to provide the features you request, such as retrieving or synchronizing game records.

2. API Keys and Account Information

API keys and other credentials entered into Airmai are used only to communicate with the third-party services selected by the user.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Airmai does not upload these credentials to servers operated by the developer and does not use them for purposes unrelated to the requested functionality.

Users are responsible for keeping their API keys and account credentials secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Airmai may interact with third-party services, including:

  • Diving-Fish
  • LXNS
  • Nearcade
  • Other third-party services explicitly selected by the user

When you use features provided through these services, relevant requests and data may be transmitted directly to those services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Airmai is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by these third-party services or the developers and publishers of the games referenced in the app, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Airmai may store certain information locally on your device, including:

  • API keys or authorization information
  • App preferences and settings
  • Cached song artwork and other media
  • Cached game records and related data
  • Practice mode settings

This information is stored primarily to provide app functionality, improve performance, and reduce unnecessary network requests.

Deleting Airmai will generally remove locally stored app data. However, information stored using certain iOS system services, such as Keychain, may be handled according to Apple's system behavior.

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Airmai is used only to provide features such as:

  • Retrieving and displaying game records
  • Synchronizing data at the user's request
  • Displaying song, arcade, and related information
  • Saving user preferences and cached content
  • Providing and improving normal app functionality

Airmai does not use your information for advertising or tracking and does not sell your personal information.

6. Data Security

Airmai takes reasonable measures to protect locally stored information and credentials and uses security features provided by iOS where appropriate.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Airmai does not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.

If you believe information has been handled in a manner inconsistent with this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Airmai's features, third-party integrations, or applicable requirements change.

Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated "Last Updated" date.
